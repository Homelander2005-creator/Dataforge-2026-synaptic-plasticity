# AI Assistance Disclosure

## Project

**Synaptic Plasticity as Short-Term Memory**

**DataForge 2026 · Pathway Track · Problem Statement Round (PS1)**

## AI tools used

AI tools were used during the development of this educational project as an assistance tool. The team remains responsible for the final content, implementation, testing, interpretation, and submission.

## How AI assistance was used

AI assistance was used for the following activities:

- Brainstorming and refining the educational concept and central claim.
- Planning the structure of the interactive learning artifact.
- Drafting and improving explanatory text for the artifact and supporting documents.
- Helping implement the HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript used by the interactive artifact.
- Suggesting interface, accessibility, responsive-layout, and visualization improvements.
- Helping debug interaction logic and revise the decay, interference, comparison, and synaptic-state visualizations.
- Helping prepare the README, concept summary, blog structure, references, and submission checklist.
- Checking citation details and helping distinguish claims supported by research from claims that belong only to the simplified educational model.
- Performing logic-level checks on the JavaScript implementation, including syntax and behavior checks.

## Human responsibility and verification

AI generated suggestions and code were reviewed and incorporated by the project team. The team is responsible for deciding which suggestions to use and for the final submitted artifact.

The interactive model is intentionally a simplified educational model. AI assistance was not treated as evidence that the toy model is biologically accurate or that it reproduces the Brain-inspired Dynamic Hardware (BDH). Research claims were checked against the cited sources and are presented with an explicit evidence boundary.

The team is responsible for independently testing the final artifact in the actual browser and submission environment, including the interactive controls, charts, responsive layout, and public deployment.

## What the AI did not establish

AI assistance does not establish:

- Biological validity of the simplified synaptic model.
- Experimental evidence for the toy model's numerical behavior.
- Equivalence between this educational artifact and the full BDH architecture.
- Any performance result for BDH or other research systems.
- Successful public deployment or competition submission.

Those points require evidence from the cited research, direct testing, or the official competition process.

## External sources and attribution

The project uses published research and technical sources listed in `REFERENCES.md`. These sources were used to ground the educational explanation and the BDH connection.

The interactive artifact itself does not use external datasets, pretrained model weights, or third-party runtime JavaScript libraries.

## Summary

AI was used as a development and writing assistant, not as a substitute for the project's research evidence, testing, or authorship responsibility. The final project team is responsible for the submitted work and its claims.
