# References

1. Adrian Kosowski, Przemysław Uznański, Jan Chorowski, Zuzanna Stamirowska, Michał Bartoszkiewicz. **The Dragon Hatchling: The Missing Link between the Transformer and Models of the Brain.** arXiv:2509.26507, 2025.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.26507
   Primary source for the BDH architecture and the claim that BDH working memory relies on synaptic plasticity with Hebbian learning.

2. Hector Garcia Rodriguez, Qinghai Guo, Timoleon Moraitis. **Short-Term Plasticity Neurons Learning to Learn and Forget.** ICML 2022, PMLR 162:1870418722.
   https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/rodriguez22b.html
   Primary ML paper on recurrent units whose synapses carry a transient state, explicitly connecting short-term plasticity with learning and forgetting.

3. Carlo S. N. Brito, Wulfram Gerstner. **Learning what matters: Synaptic plasticity with invariance to second-order input correlations.** PLOS Computational Biology 20(2), 2024.
   https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011844
   Primary research on computational synaptic plasticity and learning rules.

4. Petia D. Koprinkova-Hristova et al. **Learning cortical hierarchies with temporal Hebbian updates.** Frontiers in Computational Neuroscience, 2023.
   https://doi.org/10.3389/fncom.2023.1136010
   Primary research on temporal Hebbian updates in hierarchical cortical modeling.

5. Björn Engdahl et al. **BDH-CQ: In-Context Learning with Recurrent Latent Reasoning.** arXiv:2608.09888, 2026.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09888
   Included for context on the broader BDH family; it is not used as evidence that the toy model reproduces BDH-CQ.

## Evidence discipline
A published paper is evidence for the claims made in that paper. The interactive demo is independent educational computation. It should not be presented as a reproduction of BDH or of the biological nervous system.
